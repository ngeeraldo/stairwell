## What shipped

A bathroom tracker on one screen: today's count with plus and minus buttons,
and a bar chart of the week that toggles to weekly averages. Minus adjusts
today only and stops at zero. Days before you started, and days still to come
this week, are left blank rather than shown as zeroes.

## Built differently

The weekly average is worked out over the days actually logged in a week
rather than over all seven, so a day the app was not opened does not count as
a day of nothing. Where a week has fewer than seven logged days, the panel
says how many.

## Open

Everything in the confirmed spec was built.

One thing to decide whether to mention: the spec says time of day was
considered and turned down, and no panel shows it — but each press does record
its timestamp, because it cannot be recovered later if a future version ever
wants it.

## Notes for the next build

`pee_events` is an append-only ledger — one row per press, `delta` of ±1, the
day's figure is `SUM(delta)`. Do not collapse it into a per-day counter; the
mis-tap case was the friend's own concern and the correction needs to stay
legible as a row.

Both `day` and `at` are load-bearing. `day` is frozen at write time from the
friend's zone; deriving it from `at` on read would re-bucket their history the
first time they travel.

The floor at zero is a subquery inside the INSERT, not a read-then-write
around it. Two presses arriving together would otherwise both pass the check.
